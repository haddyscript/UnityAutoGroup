## PROJECT GOAL

Develop a modern, customer-facing mobile automotive repair website for Unity Auto Group / AutoRepairNearHere.com that allows customers to begin the vehicle repair estimating and service-request process directly through the website.

The website should be professional, mobile-friendly, simple to navigate, and designed to move customers efficiently from identifying their vehicle and repair need to receiving an estimate and requesting service.

## CUSTOMER WORKFLOW

The customer should be able to:

1. Visit AutoRepairNearHere.com.
2. Enter vehicle information.
3. Select the automotive repair or service needed.
4. Have the system identify the appropriate vehicle and repair information.
5. Access approved third-party automotive information for parts and labor.
6. Identify applicable parts, pricing, and availability.
7. Combine applicable parts and labor information to create an estimated repair quote.
8. View or receive the estimated quote.
9. Submit a service request.
10. Provide customer contact and vehicle information.
11. Have the quote and service request delivered to Unity Auto Group.
12. Allow Jeff or Unity Auto Group to review the request, obtain necessary parts, contact the customer, and schedule the mobile repair.
13. Have a Unity Auto Group technician travel to the customer and perform the repair.
